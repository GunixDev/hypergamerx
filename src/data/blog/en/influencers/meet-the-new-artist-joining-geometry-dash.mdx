## Who is MDK?
MDK, also known as Morgan David King, is an electronic music producer. He was born on October 13, 1991, in Canada. His music has been widely used by YouTubers, including the famous YouTuber 'DanTDM,' during its 'golden age.' He became part of the <Ref url="criticas/geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> community due to the use of his songs in the main levels of the game, showcasing significant growth as a producer.

## The Announcement by RobtopGames
On February 17, 2024, RobtopGames' official YouTube channel revealed the second artist to be featured in the music library of <Ref url="criticas/geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/>. This greatly benefits the community, enabling them to create new and improved levels. Music plays a significant role in <Ref url="criticas/geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> levels, and the video also features some of the artist's songs in the background.
