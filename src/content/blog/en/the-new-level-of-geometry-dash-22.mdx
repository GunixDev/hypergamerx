## Gameplay
The gameplay of the new level in <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2, called "Dash" introduces new objects and features from version 2.2. Its gameplay is very frenetic and follows the style of RobtopGames. It includes 3 coins that are well hidden, and you won't be able to see them easily. This level, upon completion, will reward you with a beautiful spider, in addition to 325 mana orbs and 12 stars. The level's difficulty is labeled as "insane," indicating it is moderately challenging, especially for beginners.

## New Effects
At this level, various new effects have been implemented in Geometry Dash, known as "Shaders." These shaders impact how we perceive the level; for instance, distortion effects and 3D/glitch effects are utilized in the level. These effects bring something unique to the game, allowing for more creative openness for level creators.

## The New Gameplay Mode
The new gameplay mode introduced in this level is called "Swing," similar to a ship but with the unique ability to change gravity mid-flight. It's a fusion of the ship and ball in Geometry Dash, adding more dynamism to the levels by introducing different vehicles.
