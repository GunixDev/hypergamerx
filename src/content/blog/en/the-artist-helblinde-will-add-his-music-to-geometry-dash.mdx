## Who is Helblinde?
Helblinde is a prolific producer and DJ born in Sweden. His hallmark is remixing anime music and video games, leaning towards electronic and trance, with a party/rave rhythm. Helblinde has traveled and performed in the production field, even winning an award at the Hardcore Heaven event in 2012.

## The Announcement by RobtopGames
On March 9th, 2024, a video was released on the RobtopGames channel introducing the fifth artist to join the Geometry Dash music library, a well-known artist with a significant career named Helblinde. This Swedish artist has added 25 songs to the game.
