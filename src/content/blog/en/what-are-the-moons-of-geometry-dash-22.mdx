## What are the moons in Geometry Dash 2.2?
The moons in <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2 are a new statistic in <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> that determines how many levels you have completed in platform mode. Every time you complete a level in the new platform mode of <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2, your moon count increases.
## What is a platform mode level?
A platform mode level is a level in which the icon can move at your discretion, jumping with different vehicles and navigating through various levels with crazy puzzles or mazes. You can move from left to right as you wish; it's not like the normal mode where you only go to the right.

## What do the moons look like?

The moons, as shown in the main image, obviously have a moon shape and are blue in color. This will be displayed in your profile, indicating the number of moons you have. It is still unknown whether this will include achievements for reaching a certain number of moons or a ranking/top list based on each player's moon count. Let's wait and see what comes out in <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2.
