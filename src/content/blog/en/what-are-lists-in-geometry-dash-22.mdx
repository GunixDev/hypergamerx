## What are lists in Geometry Dash 2.2?
Lists in <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2 offer players the possibility to organize and explore a wide variety of levels created by the community. With the arrival of the 2.2 update, these lists become an invaluable resource for those looking to discover new creations and challenges within Geometry Dash.

Lists are compilations of levels curated by the community. They can encompass a wide range of themes, difficulties, and gameplay styles, allowing players to enter a list of levels with a variety of unique experiences. From easy and simple levels to extreme action-packed ones, lists offer something for every type of player.

One of the standout features of lists is the ability for players to create their own custom compilations. This means you can select your favorite levels and organize them into a personalized list, share it with other players, or simply keep it for yourself. This functionality provides greater freedom and customization, allowing you to create collections of levels that suit your individual preferences and tastes.

## What are these lists for?
Lists in <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2 offer players a variety of levels and opportunities to earn valuable rewards. Beyond just organizing levels, these lists help you earn diamonds and stars in Geometry Dash.

One of the main functions of rated lists is to grant diamonds to players upon completing a certain number of levels included in the list. This mechanic motivates players to explore and overcome various levels, from those presenting simpler challenges to the most demanding and complex ones. Additionally, these lists can be ranked according to their difficulty, with greater difficulty yielding more rewards. This ranking system allows players to select challenges according to their skill and preferences.
