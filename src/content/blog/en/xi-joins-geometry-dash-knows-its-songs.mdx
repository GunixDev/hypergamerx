## Who is Xi?
Xi is a composer and music producer, best known for creating electronic music, particularly in genres like speedcode, trance, and other forms of high-energy rhythmic music. Xi has gained popularity within the gaming community, especially for his contributions to Geometry Dash, where many of his songs, like Freedom Dive and Blue Zenith, are recognized for their fast and challenging rhythms. Xi is active on platforms like YouTube, Spotify, and SoundCloud, where he has amassed a large following.

## The RobtopGames Announcement
On March 23, 2024, RobTop, the creator of Geometry Dash, announced the inclusion of composer Xi as the sixth artist to officially join the Geometry Dash music library. Players can use 46 of his songs when creating levels, and the community has received them positively, as Xi's songs are known for their frantic energy and rhythm, offering new possibilities.
