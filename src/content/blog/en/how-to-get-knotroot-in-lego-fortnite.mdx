## What Tool Do You Need to Get Knotroot?
Let's start by discussing the necessary tools to obtain this valuable material for our progress. You will need an upgraded crafting bench at the 'uncommon' level. With this bench, you can craft the uncommon rarity wooden axe, also known as the forest axe, using shells obtained from crabs in the desert or around a mine. With this uncommon rarity axe or a higher rarity axe, you can obtain knotroot.

## Where Can You Find Knotroot?
Knotroot can be found in the caves of the Prairie biome, one of the biomes you may start in. These caves are easy to enter and provide initial materials. In this cave, you should look for light brown roots with some green parts; this is knotroot, which you can use for your progress in the world of Lego Fortnite.
