## What pickaxe do I need to obtain Amber?
In Lego Fortnite, there are several types of pickaxes, mainly varying in their quality. Some pickaxes can be used to mine specific materials; for example, a pickaxe of rarity ("rare") can mine <Ref url="how-to-get-obsidian-in-fortnite-lego" text="obsidian"/> and copper, and it also works with other materials.

In our case, to mine amber, you first need an uncommon pickaxe. With this, you can mine <Ref url="how-to-get-amber-in-lego-fortnite" text="amber"/> in the easiest and slowest way possible. If you want to improve how quickly you can mine amber, you could obtain a rare pickaxe with a higher rarity level, which is even better. However, to start obtaining amber, you use the uncommon pickaxe. To craft the common pickaxe, you need 3 Rootrod Sticks and 3 bones, which you place in the level 2 crafting bench.
## Where can I find Amber?
In Lego Fortnite, there is only one location to find this material, which is the biome of the Dry Valley. Here, you can encounter different creatures that may pose a threat, but they also provide the opportunity to obtain the famous amber. The <Ref url="how-to-get-amber-in-lego-fortnite" text="amber"/> is located on the plateaus within this biome. These minerals have a yellowish color and are situated in the middle to upper parts of the plateaus. You can use stairs to ascend and feel comfortable while mining amber. Additionally, you can use Geysers (water holes that lift you into the sky) to obtain this <Ref url="how-to-get-amber-in-lego-fortnite" text="amber"/> mineral, but it is not highly recommended.
