## What is copper in Lego Fortnite?
Copper in Lego Fortnite is a strong mineral and a very valuable resource for your progress in Lego Fortnite. This copper mineral has a medium reddish color and will show a blue glow, indicating it is a "rare" rarity item. In your inventory, you can have a maximum of 30 copper, meaning you can't carry much unless you have ample space. However, the location where it is found is usually far from where you appear in Lego Fortnite, so we recommend not carrying too many items on your journeys.

## What is copper used for in Lego Fortnite?
Copper in Lego Fortnite is very useful for progressing in the game. Primarily, it is used to improve some tools; for example, the long sword is crafted with copper, and the forest axe is crafted with copper. This highlights its importance for your progress. It is also used for various aspects of the game that will aid you, such as creating a furnace. Copper is required to make the furnace, which, in turn, allows you to cook better and more useful meals.

## Where to find copper in Lego Fortnite?
To obtain copper in Lego Fortnite, you first need to locate a Dry Valley biome, where heat will be one of the major challenges. In this biome, you must search for a cave where it's even hotter. Once inside, look for the walls of this cave for a type of very pointed mineral, with a medium orange-purple color and some rock at its base. It can be easily found if you can build inside the cave, but be cautious as there are enemies that throw bombs, giving you a significant disadvantage when trying to collect this precious mineral.
