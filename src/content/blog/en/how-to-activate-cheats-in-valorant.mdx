## How to Activate Cheats in Valorant
Activating cheats in Valorant is limited to custom matches. Here's a step-by-step guide on how to enable and use cheats in these settings:

**Step 1: Start Valorant and Create a Custom Match**

**Go to the Play tab:** Once you are on the main menu, click on the "Play" tab located at the top of the screen.

**Create a custom match:** In the "Play" tab, you will see various game modes. Select the "Custom Match" option. This allows you to set up a match with custom settings.

**Configure your match:** You can customize various aspects of the game, such as the map, team size, and game mode. To enable cheats, click on the "Options" button, which will display a list of game options.

**Enable cheats:** Within the game options, you will find the "Cheats" option. Activate this option to enable cheats in your custom match.

**Start the game:** After enabling the cheats, proceed to start the custom match.

**Step 2: Using Cheats in the Game**

Once you are in the custom match with cheats enabled, you can access a variety of cheat commands:

**Open the menu:** To access the cheats, press the "Escape" key to open the game menu. Here you will find the "Cheats" tab.

**Activate or deactivate cheats:** In the "Cheats" tab, you can toggle different cheats on or off.

**Deactivating cheats:** If you want to disable cheats during the game, return to the "Cheats" tab and disable the options.
