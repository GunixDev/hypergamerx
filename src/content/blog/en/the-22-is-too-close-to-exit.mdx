## Why is Geometry Dash 2.2 close?
Well, for a long time, Robtop has been saying that it's very close. The 2.2 update was announced in October 2023, where the release of <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2 was expected. Many people from the community and even influencers were eagerly anticipating this update. However, what was expected did not happen, and Robtop had to delay the release of <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2 due to serious errors, which caused significant delays. He mentioned that it would be fully released by November. Now, on the 26th of November 2023, we are on the brink of finishing November, and Robtop addressed this situation.
## Robtop's Announcement
At the end of November 2023, Robtop announced through his social media channels, such as Facebook, Twitter, among others, that 2.2 is completed. All the serious issues have been resolved, thanks to the 2.2 betas. However, due to application verifications on different platforms, especially Android, where the verification process takes too long, the release of 2.2 will be delayed. ## When will Geometry Dash 2.2 be released?

As of today, there is no specific date for the release of <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2, but there are many rumors suggesting that version 2.2 might be released on December 2nd or 3rd, which are weekends. This would be a good option for the launch of <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2. Additionally, when the developer intended to release version 2.2 in October, it was planned for a weekend, specifically on Sunday, October 22, 2023. Instead of releasing the update, a trailer was unveiled, showcasing everything that the <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2 update would bring.
