## Who is Extra Terra?
Extraterra is a producer and sound designer known for several genres, including Sci-fi and Cyberpunk music, which are among the most influential in his songs. He hails from France and was born on July 18th. His real name is "Boris Hoechstetter."
## The Announcement from RobtopGames
On March 2nd, 2024, Robtop uploaded a video on his channel introducing the fourth artist revealed for the Geometry Dash music library, named Extraterra. This artist, from France, added 49 songs to the Geometry Dash library.
