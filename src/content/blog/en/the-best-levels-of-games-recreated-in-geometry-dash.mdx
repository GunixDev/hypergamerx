## Why do they recreate games in Geometry Dash?
Recreating games in [Geometry Dash](geometry-dash) is an activity that has gained popularity thanks to the new possibilities offered by the update of [Geometry Dash](geometry-dash) 2.2. This feature allows players to take creativity one step further by adapting and recreating their favorite games within the <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> universe.
With the addition of new tools and elements in the 2.2 update, such as new objects and gameplay mechanisms, creators have more options to bring their ideas to life and adapt the gameplay of known games, being able to adapt them to Geometry Dash. From platform levels inspired by classics in video games, the <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> community has demonstrated exceptional skill in recreating varied and exciting gaming experiences.
## Five Night At Freddy's in Geometry Dash
A level inspired by Five Nights at Freddy's has been created within <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2. This level brings with it the tense and suspenseful atmosphere characteristic of the famous horror franchise. The creators have managed to capture the essence of the original game by including jumpscares and a custom menu with options included for mobile players, making this level an experience of the terrifying nights in the pizzeria.
The most impressive aspect of this level is the way they have managed to recreate the feeling of a 3D game within <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> which is 2D. From the ability to view cameras to the ability to close doors to protect yourself from the animatronics, every detail has been carefully designed to offer an authentic and thrilling experience.

## Mario Bros in Geometry Dash
With the new platform mode of <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> 2.2, the <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> community is very creative, and this community has brought to life an exciting and nostalgic experience inspired by the classic game of Mario Bros. This recreation of the iconic Nintendo title offers players the opportunity to immerse themselves in a Mario Bros world where they can collect coins, accumulate points, and even enter a pipe.
Just like in the original game, players will also find important and iconic elements of Mario Bros such as the red mushroom, which allows you to grow, and the fire flower that lets you shoot fire at enemies.

## The Impossible Game in Geometry Dash
In <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/>, there are levels that are too difficult, as in all games where levels can be created, from complex levels to recreations of legendary games. A combination of both aspects is a standout level, inspired by "The Impossible Game".
This level, which pays homage to the legendary game "The Impossible Game", is known for its extreme difficulty and obstacles that are hard to overcome. Like its original counterpart, this level demands skill, precision, and patience from players to overcome each obstacle and progress towards the goal, collecting all the levels, just like the original game.
What makes this level even more challenging is its designation as a demon level in Geometry Dash. This classification indicates that it is one of the most difficult levels in the game, reserved only for the most skilled and dedicated players. Those who venture into this challenge must be prepared to face a true test of skill and determination.
