## What does the Ruby mean in Lego Fortnite?
The Ruby in Lego Fortnite is incredibly important within the game world as it represents a valuable resource. This mineral, of great rarity and difficult access, is distinguished by its characteristic intense red color. To obtain the Ruby, players must face an even greater challenge than that of common minerals. It requires the use of a pickaxe of a rarity level rare or higher.
Once players manage to obtain the coveted Ruby, new opportunities and possibilities open up in the game, as this material can be used for the creation of new and improved tools of great value and power, granting those who possess it a significant advantage in their progress in the game.
## Where can I find the Ruby in Lego Fortnite? 
In Lego Fortnite, the search for the precious Ruby requires knowledge of places such as the desert biome. It is in the depths of the volcanic caves where this mineral resides, hidden among the rocks and darkness of the underground galleries. The volcanic caves are inhabited by skeletons and are prepared to defend their territory. Venturing into the volcanic caves also involves dealing with the intense heat emanating from the depths of the earth, an additional level of challenge to the search for the Ruby, testing the resilience and determination of those who venture on this dangerous journey.
