## Who is Camellia?
Camellia, also known as 大箭将也 (Birth Name), is a VOCALOID music producer, as well as one of the Japanese EDM artists. Born on September 20, 1992, he became one of the main composers in the Sound Voltex series.

## RobtopGames Announcement
On February 10, 2024, on the same RobtopGames channel, a very unexpected short video was uploaded by the <Ref url="geometry-dash" text="Geometry Dash"/> community, in which the artist's name is announced in an extraordinary manner, showcasing some of his songs.
